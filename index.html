Fall Block

5 days, about an hour each.

Monday — Upper

Superset 6 with 7, and 8 with 9.

  1. Incline DB press3 × 6–10
  2. Pull-ups3 × max
    Add weight once set 1 hits 12+
  3. Cable or chest-supported row3 × 8–12
    Full stretch each rep
  4. Machine shoulder press2–3 × 8–12
  5. Pec deck2–3 × 10–15
  6. Single-arm cable curl2–3 × 10–15
    Left first
  7. Single-arm pressdown2–3 × 10–15
    Left first
  8. Cable lateral raise3 × 12–20
  9. Rear delt fly2 × 15–20

Short on time: 1–5 at 2 sets, then one arm superset.

Tuesday — Lower A

Warm up with your skips and leg swings. Jumps first. Squat and RDL stop 1–2 reps short of failure.

  1. Box jump or broad jump3 × 3
    Max effort, full rest, step down
  2. Isometric push into pins3 × 5 sec
    Bar under the safeties around parallel, push as hard as you can
  3. Back squat4 × 4–6
  4. Romanian deadlift3 × 6–8
    Slow on the way down
  5. Leg extension2 × 12–15
  6. Calf raise on hack squat3 × 8–12
    Pause at the bottom
  7. Hanging leg raise2 × 10–15

Short on time: jumps, squat, RDL, calves.

Wednesday — Push

On bench, stop at a rep you know you'll make. Everything else can go to failure.

  1. Barbell bench8 / 6 / 4–6
    Go heavier on the top set once you hit 6
  2. Incline machine or Smith press2–3 × 8–12
  3. Seated DB shoulder press2–3 × 8–10
  4. Cable fly2–3 × 12–15
  5. Lateral raise3 × 12–20
  6. Rope pressdown2–3 × 10–15
  7. Single-arm overhead cable extension2–3 × 10–15
    Left first

Short on time: bench, incline, laterals, pressdown at 2 sets.

Thursday — Pull

Back first, arms last.

  1. T-bar or chest-supported row3 × 6–10
  2. Weighted pull-up or lat pulldown3 × 6–10
  3. Single-arm cable or DB row2–3 × 10–12
    Left first · let the shoulder stretch forward
  4. Cable lat pullover2–3 × 12–15
  5. Face pull + cable lateral2 × 15–20 each
    Superset
  6. Single-arm preacher curl2–3 × 8–12
    Left first
  7. Hammer or forearm curl2 × 12–15

Short on time: 1–4 at 2 sets, then preachers.

Friday or Saturday — Lower B

Move fast on 1 and 3. If you played hard pickup the day before, skip the landings and depth jumps.

  1. Power clean or trap-bar jump4 × 3
    Add weight only if the bar stays fast
  2. Drop landing2 × 3
    Step off a 12–24 in box and land quiet and stiff. Hold 2 sec. Last rep each set on one leg (alternate)
  3. Depth jump, 12–18 in box3 × 3
    Step off, land, jump right away
  4. Bulgarian split squat3 × 6–10 / leg
    Left first · 1–2 reps short of failure
  5. Leg curl3 × 8–12
    3 seconds on the way down
  6. Cable or band knee drive2 × 8 / side
    Drive the knee up fast, stand tall
  7. Seated calf raise3 × 12–15

Short on time: cleans, split squats, leg curl.

Rules

Effort

  • Upper: last set to failure is fine. Not on barbell bench.
  • Squat, RDL, split squat: 1–2 reps short.
  • Jumps and cleans: stop the set if height or speed drops.
  • "2–3 sets" means 2 is fine on a busy day.

Progression

Once every set reaches the top of the rep range, go heavier next time. If a lift stalls for 2 weeks, drop 10% and build back up.

Left arm

Start single-arm sets with the left. The right does the same number of reps.

Logging

Log each set as weight × reps, e.g. 195×6, 195×5.

Pickup

Pickup counts as jumping. Hard pickup the day before Lower B → skip the depth jumps. Knee pain above 3/10 → skip that day's jumps and still lift.
